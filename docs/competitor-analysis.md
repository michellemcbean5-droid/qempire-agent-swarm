## Top 5 Competitors

### 1. **Make (formerly Integromat)**
- **Features**: Visual automation builder, 1000+ app integrations, scenario templates
- **Monetization**: Free (1,000 ops/mo), Core ($9/mo), Pro ($16/mo), Enterprise
- **Weaknesses**: No AI content generation, steep learning curve, no business plan generation
- **Our Advantage**: AI-powered business plan + website + pitch deck generation in one flow

### 2. **Zapier**
- **Features**: 5,000+ app integrations, AI-powered workflows (Zapier AI)
- **Monetization**: Free (100 tasks/mo), Professional ($19.99/mo), Team ($69/mo), Enterprise
- **Weaknesses**: Expensive at scale, no end-to-end business building, limited AI
- **Our Advantage**: Complete business buildout (not just integrations), one-time pricing option

### 3. **Manus AI**
- **Features**: Autonomous agent that browses web, writes code, generates content
- **Monetization**: Invite-only beta, pricing TBD
- **Weaknesses**: Not available publicly, no mobile app, no monetization infrastructure
- **Our Advantage**: Production-ready, mobile-first, monetized from day one

### 4. **Bubble.io**
- **Features**: No-code web app builder, visual programming, database
- **Monetization**: Free (development), Starter ($29/mo), Growth ($119/mo), Enterprise
- **Weaknesses**: Complex learning curve, no AI automation, no business strategy generation
- **Our Advantage**: AI does the work for you — no learning curve needed

### 5. **Notion + AI**
- **Features**: Docs, wikis, databases, AI writing assistant
- **Monetization**: Free, Plus ($8/mo), Business ($15/mo), Enterprise
- **Weaknesses**: Not a business builder, no automation orchestration, no website deployment
- **Our Advantage**: End-to-end business automation + deployment pipeline
